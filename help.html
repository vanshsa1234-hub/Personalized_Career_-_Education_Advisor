📚 Getting Started

Welcome to Path Finder! Here's how to make the most of our platform:

Creating Your Account

  • Click on "Register" to create a new account
  • Fill in your details including current class level
  • Verify your email to activate your account
  • Complete your profile for personalized recommendations

Taking the Aptitude Test

  • Navigate to the "Aptitude Test" section
  • Select your current class (10th or 12th)
  • Answer all 25 questions honestly and thoughtfully
  • Review your results and recommended career paths
  • Save or share your results for future reference

🎯 Understanding Your Results

Your aptitude test results provide valuable insights into your strengths and interests:

Score Interpretation

Your scores indicate your aptitude in different areas like logical reasoning, verbal ability, and numerical skills.

Career Recommendations

Based on your scores, we suggest suitable career paths in science, commerce, arts, or other fields.

Personalized Roadmap

Get a customized step-by-step plan to achieve your career goals with timelines and milestones.

Resource Suggestions

Access curated study materials, books, and online courses tailored to your recommended path.
